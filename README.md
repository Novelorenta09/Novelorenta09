## Hi, I'm Nove Lorenta👋

#### A UI/UX Designer and going forward a Data Analyst 

I see UI/UX as more than designing interfaces. I combine user research, data, and product thinking to understand user behavior, uncover insights, and turn them into recommendations that help businesses and digital platforms make better decisions.
I'm interested in combining **user research and data** to 
understand not only what users experience, but also what the 
data tells us about their behavior.

#### 🐱‍🏍What I do

- User Research & Usability Testing
- UX & Product Analysis
- User Flow, Wireframing & Prototyping
- Turning insights into actionable recommendations
