## Hi, I'm Nove Lorenta👋

#### A UI/UX Designer and going forward a Data Analyst 

I see UI/UX as more than designing interfaces. For me, good product decisions start with understanding users, 
their behavior, and the problems behind the interface. Through research, data, and product thinking, I turn findings 
into insights and recommendations that can help businesses 
and digital platforms make better decisions.
I'm interested in combining **user research and data** to 
understand not only what users experience, but also what the 
data tells us about their behavior.

#### 🐱‍🏍What I do

- User Research & Usability Testing
- UX & Product Analysis
- User Flow, Wireframing & Prototyping
- Turning insights into actionable recommendations



###  ✨My approach
Research → Insight → Recommendation → Design
